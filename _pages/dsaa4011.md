<p class="c-meta"><b>HKUST (Guangzhou) · Fall 2026–27</b> · Instructor: Yuwen Huang<br>
Lectures: Thursday 9:00–11:50, Room 233, W1 · Laboratory: Friday 15:00–15:50, Room 233, W1</p>

How can a model use local structure and observations to reach reliable global conclusions? The course represents probability models with factors, computes exact beliefs by passing local messages, and turns to information, sampling, learning and approximation when exact inference is impractical. Web slides open in the browser: use → / ← to step, F for full screen, H for keys.
